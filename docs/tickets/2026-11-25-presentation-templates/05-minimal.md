# 05: Minimal: fourteen roles, rendered and checked

**What to build:** the Minimal template complete: the twelve shared slide roles and its own two (`bignum` and `quote`), in a dark and a light shell. Large type and white space, for a talk: a statement cover, a section divider that carries one oversized number, one figure that fills a slide, a quote, and few elements on any slide. Every layout is rendered and looked at.

**Blocked by:** 02: One new slide role end to end.

**Status:** ready-for-agent
