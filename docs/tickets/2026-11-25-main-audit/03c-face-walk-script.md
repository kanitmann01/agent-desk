# 03c: The face walk script

**What to build:** the one walk the audit still lacks a tool for. Ticket 04 (#326) asks for a face walk: at least one photograph of the agent's face in each of its eight moods in each of the three themes at width 1280 (24 in all), plus the collapsed rail, the phone strip, the two dialogs and the reduced-motion poses for the eight moods. It does not ask for the full review-sheet matrix, for both motion modes on the real app, or for a server-rendered page. This ticket goes further in two places, because the walk pre-flight note recommended it: the review sheet in both motion modes, and one server-rendered page. The layout audit has no mode for it, and no instrument in the repository produces the face walk. This ticket adds `tools/face_walkthrough.py`. It reuses the layout audit's own boot, mock model, seed and request guard, so the inert capability runners are always injected, and it adds the dev-only review sheet for the one mood that no real action reaches. It records what is on the page. A surface with no face is listed by name and never filled in. This ticket was added to the audit on 2026-11-26, from the walk pre-flight note.

**Blocked by:** #346 (audit-3a), whose boot it uses. Ticket 04's face walk is blocked by this ticket while its other four passes are not. It does not change `tools/layout_audit.py`, so the BEFORE and AFTER numbers of the other walks keep one instrument.

## The actions on the real app

| Mood | The action | Desk condition and mock delay | The check before the photograph |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `idle` | Boot, sign in, land on the dashboard with nothing active and no seeded waiting cards | fresh, no delay | the rail face is `idle` |
| `listening` | Focus the research question field on the dashboard | fresh, no delay | the rail face is `listening` |
| `thinking` | Type a question and start Research, or run a saved ask; let the research request reach the desk, where the loopback mock model holds its answer for 12000 ms | full, mock delay 12000 ms | the rail face is `thinking` while the request is in flight |
| `reading` | On a seeded non-fresh plugin card, open its widget options menu and choose Re-test now; hold the marketplace retest request in the instrument's own route and never answer it | full, no delay | the rail face is `reading` while the retest is held |
| `working` | No real action reaches it. The review sheet only. | none | the sheet face is `working` |
| `waiting` | Boot the full seed and land on the dashboard; the sample data creates waiting inbox cards | full, no delay | the rail face is `waiting`, and its tooltip names the count |
| `blocked` | Type a research question; the instrument answers the research request with a 500 | full, no delay | the rail face is `blocked`, and the answer area keeps the failure and the typed words |
| `done` | Let the research action that proved `thinking` succeed | full, mock delay 12000 ms | the rail face is `done`, photographed inside its hold |
