# T10: Desk health and the brief

Part of specs/004-personal-agent-plugins.md.

## Context

`PluginWidgetRunner` is constructed without `desk_state`, so the four admin cards are blank on every real read. They are one job now: Desk health, with sections for failures and updates, policy checks, and recent audit events. The old ids stay as aliases and do not render a second card. Morning Brief and Weekly Review are one Brief plugin with a cadence of daily, weekly, or both. A missing member is omitted. The text does not contain a plugin id.

## Scope

- One plugin, `desk-health`, with three sections fed by host callbacks: runtime (failures and available updates), policy (checks and the mode), and audit (recent events, capped at 8).
- `audit-digest`, `compliance-posture`, `plugin-health`, and `update-radar` are aliases. They do not render a second card. An update opens Desk health.
- A missing callback says "Desk health is not available." An empty audit log says "No audit events in this window."
- One plugin, `brief`. Cadence is daily, weekly, or both. The user checks the instances that feed it. The text is a local template. A missing member omits its sentence. The card is blank only when every member is blank. No plugin id appears in the text. No model call.
- `morning-brief` aliases Brief at daily. `weekly-review` aliases Brief at weekly.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With the callbacks unset, Desk health shows "Desk health is not available." and no sample number.
- [ ] With fixture callbacks, the one card shows failures, policy checks, and audit events as three sections.
- [ ] Updating `audit-digest`, `compliance-posture`, `plugin-health`, or `update-radar` opens Desk health and does not add a second card.
- [ ] A daily brief with calendar connected and mail missing mentions the next event and does not mention mail or a plugin id.
- [ ] A brief whose every member is blank names the first missing connection and shows no count.
- [ ] Cadence both renders the day block and the week block on the same card.

## Tests

- New callback fixtures in `tests/test_plugins_operations.py` and `tests/test_plugins_command.py`.
- A server test that `PluginWidgetRunner` is constructed with the three callbacks.
