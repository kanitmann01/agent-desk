# T8: Time and commitment plugins (5)

Part of #97.

## Context

Calendar and commitment cards are the daily habit. They must survive a missing bridge, a stale bridge, and a partial week, because calendar data is the source most likely to be absent on day one.

## Scope

| id | Question | Source | Lead element |
|---|---|---|---|
| `calendar-today` | What is left today? | calendar-read | metric + list |
| `week-load` | Is next week over-committed? | calendar-read | metric + sparkline |
| `meeting-prep` | What do I need before the next meeting? | calendar-read | timeline + checklist |
| `follow-up-watch` | Which promises are aging? | csv-watch | list |
| `renewal-radar` | What hits its notice deadline in 30 days? | excel-watch | list |

- `calendar-today` is an upgrade of the existing plugin; keep its id, add the v2 fields, and keep the day-briefing skill.
- `renewal-radar` keys on the notice deadline, not the renewal date, and sorts by days remaining.
- `follow-up-watch` computes age from the commitment date and flags anything past its promised date.
- `meeting-prep` reads only the next event's metadata and the notes checklist; it never invents attendees or agenda.
- Categories `time` (calendar and commitments), tags for the job.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All five validate as v2 and appear in the bundled catalog.
- [ ] Each executes against fixtures and returns the documented lead value with as-of and state.
- [ ] `calendar-today` keeps its id and installs over 0.1.0.
- [ ] `renewal-radar` sorts by notice deadline and labels the deadline column as notice, not renewal.
- [ ] A missing calendar bridge renders blank with the bridge setting name; a stale bridge renders stale with the last sync date.
- [ ] `week-load` renders a 12 to 30 point sparkline from the fixture and never more than 30.
- [ ] No plugin reads outside `${workspace}/` or the calendar bridge path.

## Tests

Registry package seam: one module for the batch with fixture calendar bridge, commitments CSV, and renewals workbook, plus blank and stale cases.

Remaining families (T9 to T11), packs (T12).
